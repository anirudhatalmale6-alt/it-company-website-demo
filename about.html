Our story

Started because the alternative was frustrating.

We spent years inside larger IT firms watching good businesses get treated as ticket numbers. Escalations that went nowhere. Invoices that arrived after an outage the provider caused. Documentation held hostage at contract renewal.

So we built the opposite: a small, deliberately capped client list, named engineers, flat monthly pricing, and every password and network diagram in an account you own. If you ever leave, you leave with everything.

That's the whole pitch. It isn't clever — it's just uncommon.

How we work

Four rules we don't bend.

These are the things clients actually notice six months in, long after the sales conversation is forgotten.

01

Plain English, always

If we can't explain a recommendation without acronyms, we don't understand it well enough to charge you for it.

02

You own everything

Licences, domains, DNS, password vault, network documentation — all in your name from day one. No hostage situations.

03

Fix the cause

Anything that breaks twice gets a root-cause write-up, not a third reboot. Recurring tickets are our problem, not yours.

04

We say no

If a project isn't in your interest, or someone else is genuinely better placed, we'll tell you and point you at them.
