How it starts

From first call to fully covered.

Onboarding takes about two weeks for a typical 25-person office, with zero planned downtime.

  1. Free IT review

    A 45-minute call and a walk of your setup. You get a written summary of what's healthy, what's risky and what's costing you money — whether or not you hire us.

  2. Fixed proposal

    One page, one number. Scope, response times and exclusions in plain English, not a 30-page PDF written by a lawyer.

  3. Onboarding

    We document everything, install monitoring, close the obvious holes and meet your team. Out of hours where it would disrupt work.

  4. Steady state

    Day-to-day support, monthly health reports and a quarterly sit-down about what's next — budget, growth, hardware refresh.
